# Nova Interiors — Project Handover SOP

Version 3.1 · Last revised 14 August 2025 · Owner: Priya Nair (Head of Operations)

This document describes how a signed project moves from sales into delivery and
how it is closed out. It applies to all residential fit-out projects. Commercial
projects follow a separate note held by the commercial team.

---

## 1. Handover from sales to delivery

1.1 Sales closes the project and records the signed quotation in Zoho Books.

1.2 The founder (or the sales lead) sends the signed quotation, the client's
contact details and the agreed scope to the Head of Operations by email.

1.3 The Head of Operations assigns a Project Manager within two working days and
records the assignment in the master project sheet (`Nova Master Tracker.xlsx`,
one tab per project).

1.4 The Project Manager creates a WhatsApp group named
`Nova – <Locality> <Configuration> – Site` and adds the site supervisor. The
client is added once civil work begins, not before — clients added during the
drawing stage tend to request changes that have not been costed.

> **Known gap.** There is no formal record of what was promised during the sales
> conversation beyond the quotation itself. Verbal commitments made on the sales
> call regularly surface later as expectations. This has been raised three times
> at the monthly review and is still open.

---

## 2. Weekly status reporting

2.1 The site supervisor posts a progress update with photographs to the project
WhatsApp group at least twice a week.

2.2 The Project Manager summarises progress in the master project sheet every
Friday, including the current milestone, the next milestone and any slippage.

2.3 The Head of Operations reviews the master sheet on Monday morning and
escalates any project that has slipped by more than five working days.

> **Known gap.** Step 2.2 is the step most often missed. During busy periods the
> sheet is reconciled fortnightly rather than weekly, and the Monday review then
> runs on stale data. No automated reminder exists.

---

## 3. Client communication

3.1 Routine progress questions are answered by the Project Manager in the
project WhatsApp group.

3.2 Any communication involving a date change, a cost change or a change of
scope must be approved by the founder before it is sent to the client.

3.3 Change requests from the client are costed by the Project Manager, approved
by the founder, and confirmed in writing in the group before work proceeds.

3.4 The group message confirming a change request is the record of that
approval. Project Managers should screenshot such confirmations and file them in
the project folder on Google Drive.

> **Known gap.** Step 3.4 is followed inconsistently. Where a client has left the
> group or a supervisor has changed handsets, the approval record has in some
> cases been lost entirely.

---

## 4. Materials and purchasing

4.1 The Project Manager raises a purchase order in Zoho Books against the
project.

4.2 The Project Manager confirms the delivery date with the vendor over
WhatsApp or telephone.

4.3 Expected delivery dates are written on the site whiteboard and communicated
verbally to the site supervisor.

4.4 If a delivery slips, the Project Manager reschedules dependent labour.

> **Known gap.** There is no shared record of expected delivery dates. Steps 4.2
> and 4.3 mean the information exists only in the Project Manager's own notes and
> on a whiteboard at the site. Slipped deliveries are typically discovered on the
> morning the dependent labour arrives, at which point the idle-day cost is
> already committed.

---

## 5. Snagging and handover

5.1 On completion, the Project Manager walks the site with the client and
records a snag list.

5.2 Snags are closed within ten working days unless a material lead time
prevents it.

5.3 On closure, the Project Manager confirms in the group and the Head of
Operations marks the project complete in the master sheet.

5.4 The project WhatsApp group is retained for thirty days after handover and is
then left dormant.

> **Known gap.** No project archive exists. Once a group is dormant, the
> photographic record of the project — which is the only visual record we hold —
> is distributed across individual participants' handsets and is effectively
> irretrievable. Requests from past clients for photographs of their completed
> project cannot currently be fulfilled.

---

## 6. Systems of record

| Information | System of record | Who maintains it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quotations, invoices, purchase orders | Zoho Books | Accounts |
| Project status and milestones | Nova Master Tracker.xlsx | Project Managers |
| Day-to-day site communication | WhatsApp (per project) | Site supervisors |
| Drawings and design files | Google Drive | Design team |
| Client approvals | WhatsApp (per project) | Project Managers |

> **Note.** Zoho Books is the sole system of record for anything financial. No
> other system should generate documents that could be mistaken for an invoice,
> and GST treatment on interior work is to be handled only by the accountant.
